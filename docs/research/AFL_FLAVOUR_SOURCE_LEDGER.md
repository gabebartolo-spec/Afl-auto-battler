# AFL flavour research — source ledger

Companion to [the report](AFL_FLAVOUR_AND_CULTURE_RESEARCH.md). Accessed 5 October 2026.

**23 external sources:** fifteen page/PDF bodies read directly; eight sources consulted through substantive indexed article text, relevant report passages or an abstract. Indexed sources are labelled below; they are not counted as full-document readings. No source is a controlled evaluation of our game. No interviews, asset licences or hands-on tests were conducted.

Selection covered language, supporter belonging, banner craft, nickname continuity, sound, ordinary Australian storytelling, regional settings, varied communities and significant occasions. The two sustained Reddit discussions supply individual experiences and counterpoints, not representative prevalence. Upvotes were not used as statistical evidence. Older sources document particular historical practices; they are not instructions to reproduce every practice in a 2027-start career.

## Evidence rejected or not promoted

Search also surfaced general slang guides, Wikipedia leads, unrelated “football” results belonging to other codes, inaccessible forum links and an oversized humour PDF. These were not promoted into evidence for recommendations. Failed direct opens above are disclosed where useful indexed text was available.

No full-paper reading is claimed for Rolfe. No visual inspection is claimed for the Footy Focus images. No permission to use cultural designs or recordings was obtained. No forum comment about a real person's private conduct is treated as fact.

## Interpretation discipline

- **Documented fact:** what a named source actually describes, with date/context where supplied.
- **Creator/practitioner account:** an explanation of choices or customs; valuable but not causal proof.
- **Critical interpretation:** a critic's reading of a work; not universal reception.
- **Supporter account:** an individual's reported experience; not prevalence or verified biography.
- **Our hypothesis:** the proposed game adaptation and its expected benefit.
- **Future evidence:** observed sessions and gameplay-neutrality checks still to be conducted.

The report uses these distinctions instead of converting source volume into a confidence score. The director subsequently included all eight FL proposals on 6 October 2026; see [roadmap §9.3](../ROADMAP.md#93-approved-flavour-and-culture-work--director-decisions-2026-10-06). Approval changes execution scope, not the strength or limits of this evidence.
