# How CI splits the Godot test suites across parallel jobs
# (.github/workflows/tests.yml). One line per shard: its number, a colon, then
# the suites it runs. Every suite in tools/run_tests.sh must appear in exactly
# one shard; tools/check_ci_shards.sh checks that on every CI run, so a new
# suite cannot quietly skip CI.
#
# Balanced on the suite times of one full green run (2026-10-06), about 400 s a
# shard against 1595 s in a row: match_game 225, career 217, match_visual 163,
# league_balance 123, balance 101, club 83, calibration 70, the rest under 55.
# A new suite goes in the shortest shard; move a suite when it gets much slower.
1: finals potential training injuries awards league match_game
2: draft_ui chronology career coach_pathway matchup matchday contracts workload calibration
3: save coaches coach_effects ratings ai selection roles club pressure match_visual
4: draft intake intake_ui expansion coach_market career_ui achievements league_balance balance
