# Professional polish benchmarks for sports management / coach sims

Scope: what critics, players and developers cite when they call a management game "polished/professional" versus "janky/spreadsheet/amateur", 2023-2026 material preferred (older marked by year). Target reader: a small Godot 4 team building a premium, portrait-first, dark-theme, flat-editorial AFL coach sim with no microtransactions.

Evidence labels used: [critic review], [player aggregate], [store page / dev statement], [dev talk], [older: year].

## Q1. What do reviewers cite when they call a management game "polished/professional" vs "janky/spreadsheet/amateur"?

### Takeaway
"Professional" is cited as: a clean, fluid interface where every fact is findable in one or two clicks, colour/logo coding with help text one tap away, a match view that marries TV-style coverage with data, reliable autosave, a strong "one more match" pull. "Janky/amateur" is cited as: cluttered or click-heavy navigation, small text, panels buried under pop-ups, UI that stops updating, tutorial messages that re-appear, misaligned/overflowing text, tiny sprites with janky animation, cartoon portraits that break the register, and launches that regress on the previous version.

### Inferences
- The single most repeated professional marker is **navigation economy**: any fact in one or two taps, no modal stacking, a stable "where am I" model. FM26's collapse in sentiment came mostly from breaking this, not from the match engine.
- **Legibility at matchday** is a specific checkpoint: can you see at a glance who is tired or injured? Compacting tiles for "modern look" was read as regression.
- **Register consistency** matters: one off-register element (cartoon portraits, rough faces, jarring over-enthusiastic commentary) is called out even when everything else passes.
- "Spreadsheet" is used for games where the primary surface is tables with no coding, no help text, no match view worth watching; "janky" is used for sprites/animation and for UI that desyncs or re-shows dismissed messages.
- A reliable autosave gets named as a polish item in its own right.

### Gaps
- No RPS/Eurogamer/IGN review text was retrievable for FM26 or F1M24; findings rest on Shacknews, GGRecon, Use a Potion, TheSixthAxis, PC Gamer via OpenCritic, and player aggregates.
- No critic review found that uses the exact word "spreadsheet" for a 2023-2026 release; the usage is older (Baseball Mogul 2009) or player-forum.

### Inferences
- A 2D/abstract match view is accepted by critics **if** the information design and tone are strong (Golden Lap, OOTP's minimalist viewer), but "boring at times" follows if nothing else carries the moment. Broadcast framing (cameras, scoreboard, commentary) is the lever F1 Manager uses to make watching feel like TV.
- Commentary is high risk: real licensed voices earn praise once, then "monotonous" and "odd delays" criticism. A small team with no voice budget should probably not attempt spoken commentary; written, varied, well-timed text is safer.
- Minimal-art games are judged on text size and controller/touch fiddliness more harshly because there is nothing else to look at.

## Q3. Genre conventions for draft night, awards night, season review, hall of fame; who does them memorably

### Takeaway
The highest-profile template is Madden NFL 25's rebuilt "Draft Night": a staged ceremony (commissioner on stage, picks in suits holding the jersey, the phone call), a live-updating board, and real-time draft grades. In the small-dev space, ZenGM's changelog shows the quieter conventions: draft status always visible, lottery page shows how close every team was, Hall of Fame table with MVP/title columns, "extremely customizable awards", and undo on signings.

### Cited Findings
- Madden NFL 25 Draft Night [publisher notes, 2024]: draft "begins when Roger Goodell takes the stage"; "the Draft Board updates in real-time"; staged moments of "your draft pick walking across the stage, holding up their new jersey or receiving the life-changing call from home"; draftees in suits not uniforms; "real-time draft grades"; prospect hub redesigned into "a more familiar spreadsheet format" for data; "more pageantry akin to what NFL fans see in real life" — [EA Gridiron Notes](https://ea.com/games/madden-nfl/madden-nfl-25/news/gridiron-notes-madden-25-franchise-and-presentation), [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/news/madden-nfl-25-franchise-and-presentation-deep-dive-notes-released), [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/madden-25-franchise-mode-features-revealed/)
- ZenGM (Basketball/Football/Baseball/Hockey GM) changelog entries [dev changelog, dated by version]: Jul 2025 "Improved draft UI - current status and buttons to advance the draft are always visible"; Apr 2025 Draft Lottery page shows non-lottery picks "so you can see how close other teams are to the lottery"; Jun 2026 "Added columns to the Hall of Fame table showing the number of MVPs and championships won"; Sep 2026 "Extremely customizable awards and many other award features"; Jul 2025 live sim "current score is always visible at the top"; Jul 2026 undo when you sign a free agent or release a re-signing player; Apr 2026 contract negotiation "as a popup rather than a separate page" — [ZenGM changelog](https://zengm.com/changelog/)
- OOTP 25: "Dynamic Evolution" changes league rules/economics over time; historical season recreation "with accurate statistics, stadiums"; player development complex gives "noticeable year-over-year changes" — [GameCritics](https://gamecritics.com/brad-bortone/out-of-the-park-baseball-25-review/)
- Hoop Land: awards are user-customisable alongside names, uniforms, courts; each save links a college and pro league so prospects flow in — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2453660/Hoop_Land/)
- Golden Lap: emergent story beats (hungover drivers, fatal-crash dialogue) are what the critic remembers, not a ceremony — [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/golden-lap-review-a-different-take-on-f1-management-from-the-art-of-rally-devs/)
- ZenGM blog explains AI behaviour in plain prose (why AI teams keep draft picks; trade AI rewrite) — [ZenGM blog 20](https://zengm.com/blog/20), [ZenGM blog 19](https://zengm.com/blog/19)

### Inferences
- The convention for a draft night is three layers: a **ceremony** (stage, name read, jersey/phone-call moment), a **live board** that updates pick by pick, and an **immediate verdict** (grade or reaction). Even a 2D game can do all three with type and motion.
- Hall-of-fame/history pages are judged by whether the headline honours (MVPs, titles) are columns, not buried in a profile.
- "Undo" on roster actions is now a shipped convention in a leading free sim (ZenGM 2026).

### Gaps
- No critic review found that evaluates an awards night or season review ceremony specifically in a management game; evidence is publisher notes (Madden) and a dev changelog (ZenGM). FM's end-of-season awards and OOTP's awards presentation were not retrievable as reviewed items.

## Q4. Expected accessibility and quality-of-life options

### Takeaway
Steam's own accessibility feature taxonomy and the Game Accessibility Guidelines name the four most-complained-about items: remapping, text size, colour-blind support, subtitle presentation. For a data-dense manager the practical set is UI scale / text size, no colour-only state, adjustable contrast, remappable or at least customisable controls, sim speed up to ~8x, reliable autosave, and undo on roster actions.

### Cited Findings
- Game Accessibility Guidelines: remapping, text size, colourblindness and subtitle presentation are "the four most commonly complained about accessibility issues" — [GAG basic](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/basic/)
- Steamworks accessibility features documentation lists adjustable text size (in-game, menu, dialogue, subtitle), customisable subtitles, "Gameplay doesn't rely on colors to communicate important information, or players have an option to adjust colors", "Contrast is adjustable" — [Steamworks docs](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/accessibility_features)
- 2025 opinion: colour-blind modes often "swap one problem color pair for another"; "Use shapes, symbols, or text cues in addition to color" — [Wayline blog, Jun 2025](https://www.wayline.io/blog/accessibility-illusion-indie-games-gone-wrong)
- Example option set (Wildgate, 2025): UI scale slider 70-150%, subtitle font size and background opacity, colour-blind correction strength slider — [Dreamhaven support](https://support.dreamhaven.com/hc/en-us/articles/49916450913435-Accessibility-Features); Cyberpunk 2.1 added a dedicated "Accessibility" settings tab — [devtrackers](https://devtrackers.gg/cyberpunk-2077/p/96ae972b-update-2-1-accessibility-features)
- Switch 2 added console-level screen reader, text resizing and zoom (Apr 2025) — [Double Tap](https://doubletaponair.com/new-nintendo-switch-2-gets-new-accessibility-options/)
- Management-genre specifics: OOTP 25 "controls are not remappable" listed as a negative — [GameCritics](https://gamecritics.com/brad-bortone/out-of-the-park-baseball-25-review/); FM26 "Text is smaller" is a complaint — [Use a Potion](https://www.useapotion.com/2025/11/football-manager-26-review-a-promising-rebuild-thats-not-quite-match-fit/); Golden Lap tiny text on 7.4-inch Deck — [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/golden-lap-review-a-different-take-on-f1-management-from-the-art-of-rally-devs/); PCM 2024 "not fully accessible" — [GameCritics](https://gamecritics.com/david-bakker/pro-cycling-manager-2024-review/); PCM 25 patch notes "added accessibility options" (unspecified) — [Vaporlens PCM25](https://vaporlens.app/app/2511310/pro_cycling_manager_25)
- Speed controls: Golden Lap races "relatively short, especially at up to 8x speed" — [Traxion](https://traxion.gg/golden-lap-review-a-different-take-on-f1-management-from-the-art-of-rally-devs/); Jimmy's Super League "Please Wait" complaint shows waiting on the sim is a decades-old irritant — [EveryGameGoing](https://everygamegoing.com/larticle/Jimmys-Super-League-000/34228)
- Autosave: "autosave system is reliable" counted as a plus in F1M24 — [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/140757/f1-manager-2024-review-we-think-it-might-be-plan-a); FM players recover corrupted saves from automatic backups in the game folder — [FM Scout thread](https://www.fmscout.com/q-3142-Save-game-cant-be-loaded.html)
- Undo: ZenGM Jul 2026 undo for signing/releasing — [ZenGM changelog](https://zengm.com/changelog/); keyboard shortcuts customisable (Feb 2026) — same source
- Platform parity as polish: F1M24 Switch "complete feature-parity" — [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/f1-manager-24-nintendo-switch/); Hoop Land still refining "full controller support and Steam Deck compatibility" in EA — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2453660/Hoop_Land/); ZenGM Nov 2024 mobile menu added for trade pages — [ZenGM changelog](https://zengm.com/changelog/)

### Inferences
- For a portrait phone sim, the checklist that critics will actually test: text size/UI scale, no colour-only state (club colours vs GOOD/BAD), contrast on dark theme, sim speed and skip, autosave with rolling backups, undo on roster actions, and no "Please Wait" dead screens.
- Cloud saves were not mentioned in any retrieved review; absence of a cloud save is unlikely to be criticised, but save loss will be.

### Gaps
- No source found listing accessibility expectations specifically for management/strategy games in 2024-2026; the above is a proxy from general guidelines plus genre reviews.
- No evidence found on haptics expectations for mobile management games.

## Q5. What successful small/solo-dev management games do in release practice that earns trust

### Takeaway
The evidence trail (ZenGM, Hoop Land, Golden Lap) shows: a public, dated changelog with blog/Reddit links per release; an Early Access page that states duration, current state, planned features, price promise and where feedback is read; free post-launch content; a generous free tier with no ads; mod support; and plain-prose explanations of AI behaviour. Save-compatibility promises were not found stated explicitly.

### Cited Findings
- ZenGM changelog: version numbers built from release date plus build (e.g. v2026.10.16.0673); grouped by year back to 2013; each entry lists sports affected, a one-line summary, links to a blog post and/or Reddit discussion; contributors credited; "Only big news" filter; several releases per month — [ZenGM changelog](https://zengm.com/changelog/)

## Q6. Is there writing on "game feel"/"juice" for simulation or management games?

### Takeaway
No dedicated writing on juice for management sims was found. General game-feel sources (Swink) and student/industry notes give principles (layered brief feedback scaled to importance; transient, not a new resting state). The closest genre-specific evidence is critics praising a strong "one more match" pull, TV-style framing, and always-visible score during live sim, and criticising interruptions that stop momentum.

### Cited Findings
- Term popularised by Steve Swink's *Game Feel*; "juiciness" is how good a game feels to play — [UAL student research blog](https://23037202.myblog.arts.ac.uk/?p=202), [Bangor CS dissertation](https://csee.bangor.ac.uk/expo/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/cs/pdfs/MaguireL.pdf)
- Principle: a satisfying event is 5-8 tiny responses within ~100 ms (sound, flash, pop, number); "exaggerate briefly and return to rest"; scale effect to importance; interface elements may respond (hover enlarge/colour) — [Agentskill juice notes](https://agentskill.sh/plugins/sponticelli/juice) (secondary source, general)
- Genre evidence of momentum as feel: F1M24 "just one more" pull — [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/140757/f1-manager-2024-review-we-think-it-might-be-plan-a); FM26 "'just one more match' pull remains strong" but pop-ups make it "tough to build momentum" — [Use a Potion](https://www.useapotion.com/2025/11/football-manager-26-review-a-promising-rebuild-thats-not-quite-match-fit/); PC Gamer: off-pitch "refuses to let time pass with any rapidity" — [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/19479/-/reviews)
- Live-sim feel conventions: ZenGM "current score is always visible at the top" — [ZenGM changelog](https://zengm.com/changelog/); Motorsport Manager race view combines TV-style map with data — [Stuff.tv](https://www.stuff.tv/game-reviews/motorsport-manager/review); F1M22 syncs gear-shift animation and audio by triggering early, and aligns pass-by audio markers with the car passing the camera — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/production/deep-dive-f1-manager-2022)
- Negative feel: commentary with "odd delays" is "jarring" — [Shacknews](https://shacknews.com/article/140757/f1-manager-2024-review-we-think-it-might-be-plan-a); "Please Wait" screens — [EveryGameGoing](https://everygamegoing.com/larticle/Jimmys-Super-League-000/34228)

### Inferences
- For a manager the equivalent of juice is **pacing feel**: no dead time, no stacked interruptions, headline number always visible, and timing-tight feedback (audio and motion in sync). Count-ups and reveals are plausible applications of "brief exaggeration scaled to importance" but no source evaluates them in this genre.

### Gaps
- No GDC talk or essay specifically on juice/game feel for management or sports sims was found in this pass.
- No source on haptics in mobile sports managers.

## Appendix: condensed benchmark checklist derived from the evidence above (each line traces to a finding)

Presentation
- Match view marries broadcast framing (score always visible, TV-style map/field, scoreboard) with data; watchable, not an afterthought (Stuff.tv MM; ZenGM; F1M).
- One visual register throughout; no element (portrait style, face quality, commentary tone) outside it (OSFTW MM; GameCritics OOTP25; Shacknews F1M24).
- Audio/visual events synced to the tick (Game Developer F1M22); spoken commentary only if it can avoid repetition and delay (Shacknews, XboxHub).

Information design
- Every fact in 1-2 taps, no modal stacking, stable back (FM26 critics).
- Colour/logo coding plus help text one tap away (OSFTW MM).
- Matchday legibility test: who is tired/injured at a glance (FM26 critics).
- Text size and UI scale options; no "Text is smaller" regressions (Use a Potion; Traxion; Steamworks).

Onboarding
- In-game tutorial that also teaches veterans something (GameCritics OOTP25); tutorial prompts never re-appear after dismissal (Shacknews F1M24); a lighter "pivotal moments only" mode lowers the entry bar (OOTP Drive for the Pennant).

Season pacing
- No interruption stacks between matches; time passes quickly when asked (PC Gamer; Use a Potion); sim speed up to ~8x and skip (Traxion).

Ceremony and persistence
- Draft: live board, ceremony beat, immediate verdict; status/advance controls always visible (EA Madden 25; ZenGM).
- Lottery/order pages show the near-misses (ZenGM).
- Hall of Fame tables carry MVPs and titles as columns; awards customisable (ZenGM).
- League evolves over years (OOTP Dynamic Evolution).

AI believability
- Explain AI behaviour in plain prose publicly (ZenGM blog); watch for exploitable AI and roster churn, the two complaints that stuck to OOTP 26 (OOTP forums; Wasdland).

QA/stability
- Reliable autosave with rolling backups (Shacknews; FM Scout); no UI desync during live sim; no overflow/misaligned text; undo on roster actions (ZenGM).

Release practice
- Dated changelog with blog/Reddit links; EA statement with duration, current state, plan, price promise, feedback channels; devlog per release; free post-launch content; mod/customisation surface; demo (Hoop Land Steam; ZenGM; StartMenu; Vaporlens "try the demo").
- Platform parity treated as a review item (Pocket Tactics/GGRecon Switch; Hoop Land Deck).
- Monetisation note: the free tier "no ads or micro-transactions" with one unlock is read as trustworthy (Hoop Land); 54% launch-year discounts on a Mixed title read as the opposite (PBM 2025).
