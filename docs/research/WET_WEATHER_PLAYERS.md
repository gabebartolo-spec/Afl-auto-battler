# Wet-weather players: what is published, by name

Evidence for ARD-M4-016's "Wet-weather player" trait on real players. Research only; no
player is listed without a source, and nothing here is a rating.

## Measured

Source: Stats Insider, "How Different Weather Conditions Affect AFL Performance" (Elliott,
8 July 2020), https://www.statsinsider.com.au/news/how-different-weather-conditions-affect-afl-performance.
fitzRoy data, 2012 to 2019: 559 players with at least 10 games in both fine and poor
conditions; 13 averaged at least one full AFLCA coaches' vote more in poor weather.

| Player | Club in the game's 2026 list | The measure, as the source states it |
|---|---|---|
| Lachie Whitfield | GWS | "difference of +2.15 votes in poor weather is the biggest in the game" |
| Patrick Dangerfield | Geelong | "average of 5.35 votes in poor weather games is the highest of any player in the comp" |
| Nat Fyfe | not on the 2026 list (retired) | "the next best" at 3.9 votes in poor weather |

The club column is the game's own 2026 list (`data/players_2026.csv`), not the source:
the article does not name clubs for these three. Fyfe is shown for completeness and
cannot be a 2026 player.

**The opposite case, same source:** Clayton Oliver's "-1.33 votes the worst of any
current player" in poor weather (GWS in the game's 2026 list). Jobe Watson's -2.32 was the
worst differential of the period, but he is retired.

## Not found

- No 2025 or 2026 published measure naming individual players. The 2025 ABC piece
  (https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-06-26/the-winter-game-how-australian-rules-football-adapts-to-weather/105462202)
  is team-level: since 2022 Collingwood won 92% of wet games, Geelong 83%, Carlton 69%
  and the Western Bulldogs 67%, against West Coast 0%, Essendon 20% and GWS 25%. Those
  are clubs, not players.
- The Stats Insider figures are about six years old. They name three current players with a
  published measure; a trait list longer than that would need fresh data (for example
  Champion Data's wet-game splits), which is not public.
