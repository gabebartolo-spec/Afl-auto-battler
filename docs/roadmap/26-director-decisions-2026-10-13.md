## 9. Believable comebacks (row 42)
"my tester reported a scenario where he was 2 goals up at 3qt, simmed the last quarter, lost by 40 points. we need to
codify realistic comeback rules. I mean a 52 point swing in one quarter is POSSIBLE but very very very unlikely. We need
to keep comebacks in the game, maybe even make them a bigger part (Read more narrative/UI elements rather than
frequency) of the game, but they need to be believable and not seem like the game hallucinating".
