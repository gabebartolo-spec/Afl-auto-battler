His words are in quotes. Where a later decision of his differs, the later one wins. Nothing here edits the
design bible. Source: [the genre comparison report](../research/SPORTS_MANAGEMENT_GENRE_COMPARISON_2026-10-18.md)

## 1. Order of work
"No. Start with Tier B (P-12, the oval answers a structural call) and fold Tier A into the 1.0 plan."
- Tier B (makes the fun land) comes first, led by P-12: a structural call (game plan, flood, stack the contest)
  visibly changes what the oval shows, proved by a seeded positional test.
- Tier A (professional baseline: save version chain and restore, export/import, three first-week walkthroughs,
  text-size setting, phone perf numbers and soak, tired/injured at a glance, interruption budget, semver and
  What's new, undo on list moves, no state by colour alone, Report a problem) is folded into
  [the 1.0 release plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-release-1-0.md), not a gate before it.

## 2. Press conferences (P-17)
"Yes, press conferences only when something happened." A presser fires on a reason the journalist can name (a
big result, a debut, a star's injury, a selection shock, a streak), never on a schedule. Fewer pressers, each with
weight. Design change to the press cadence; the answer-to-effect rules are unchanged.

## 3. Key moments (P-14)
"Yes, add Key moments beside Watch and Skip." A third way to take a match: it runs itself and stops for the coach's
own decisions, goals and the quarter breaks. Playback only; the sim is unchanged.

## 4. Difficulty dials (P-20)
"Yes, two named difficulty dials; I set the sizes in playtest." Two dials the player can read, how hard the
opposition coaches play against you and how tough the board is on you. Agents build the dials and the copy; the
sizes are his, set in his playtest (balance stays his call).

## 5. Public demo page (P-23)
"Yes, pursue the public demo page before 1.0." Against the report's recommendation of "not yet"; his call. Rights
to real names and the licence register come before anything public (release plan Phase 0 interview).
