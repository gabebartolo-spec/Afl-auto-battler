## Reading

- **No single lever reaches the target of one flag in 8–10 seasons.** That
  rate would be 4–5 flags in 40 seasons. Draft gets 4 and base gets 3. With 40
  seasons, a gap of 1–2 flags is noise (a binomial SD of about 1.6).
- **Match-day calls are the clearest lever, but they lift finishes, not
  flags.** Calls give the best mean ladder (7.1 against 9.6 for base) and 13
  top-4 finishes, but only 2 flags.
- **Only "all" holds the list up.** Its year-5 rank is 5.9, against 7.4 for
  base, and its ladder in years 4–5 is about 6–7, where base is about 10–11.
  This is the only variant where the autopilot slide is visibly arrested by
  year 5.
- **The development lever does little.** Learning projects are only started in
  years 1–2. From year 3 on there are 0 active projects, because
  `learnable_jobs` offers nothing new. Year-5 rank is worse than base (8.9),
  because high performance and football drop to Minimal. Dev's year-1 ladder
  of 5.4 is only noise: the budget doesn't apply until year 2.
- **The draft lever does produce the year-5 rank-1 lists** (as does "all").
  Its effect is slow, as you'd expect, so 5 seasons understates it.

## Where flag winners come from now

This is the premier's list-strength rank at the start of its flag season,
pooled over all 200 seasons (5 levers × 40):

| start rank | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5–8 | 9–12 | 13–18 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| premiers | 36 (18%) | 24 (12%) | 25 (12.5%) | 19 (9.5%) | 48 (24%) | 24 (12%) | 24 (12%) |

52% of premiers start in the top 4, and 24% start from 9th or lower. The flag
is open: the best list wins about 1 in 5. This explains why any lever moves
flags weakly. Even a top-3 list converts only about 1 time in 7 or 8.

## Caveats

- These are 40-season samples, so flag counts carry about ±1.6 noise. The
  ladder and rank columns are steadier.
- The dev lever's learning stops after year 2. A harness that re-offers jobs
  could change dev's result a little, but not its rank trend.
- calls uses one simple rule (counter the plan the opposition ran last
  quarter). A human reading the match could do better.
